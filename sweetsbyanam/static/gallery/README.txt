Put photos of your bakes in this folder (.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp).
They appear automatically on the Gallery page and in the "Recent bakes" strip on the home page, in
alphabetical order by file name (name them 01-cake.jpg, 02-cupcakes.jpg ... to control the order).

Tip: shrink photos to about 1200 pixels wide first so the site loads fast.
Photos are shown as tall arches (4:5), so portrait-style photos with the treat in the middle look best.
